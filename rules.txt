Blackjack Rules and How to Play

Objective
The goal of blackjack is to beat the dealer by having a hand worth more points than the dealer’s hand without exceeding 21.

Card Values
- Number cards (2–10) are worth their face value.
- Jacks, queens, and kings are worth 10 points.
- Aces are worth either 1 or 11 points, whichever is better for the hand.

How to Play
1. Place a bet before the cards are dealt.
2. Each player receives two cards. The dealer also receives two cards, usually one face up and one face down.
3. If your first two cards are an ace and a 10-value card, you have a “blackjack.” A blackjack normally beats any other hand totaling 21.
4. On your turn, choose from the available actions:
   - Hit: Take another card.
   - Stand: Keep your current hand and end your turn.
   - Double down: Double your original bet, receive exactly one more card, and then stand.
   - Split: If your first two cards have the same value, divide them into two hands and place an equal bet on the second hand.
   - Surrender: At tables that allow it, give up the hand and lose half your bet.
5. If your hand exceeds 21, you “bust” and immediately lose.
6. After all players finish, the dealer reveals the hidden card. The dealer must hit until reaching at least 17, according to the table’s rules.
7. If the dealer busts, all remaining players win. Otherwise, hands are compared:
   - A hand closer to 21 than the dealer’s wins.
   - A hand lower than the dealer’s loses.
   - Equal totals result in a “push,” and the player’s original bet is returned.

Payouts and Table Rules
- A normal winning hand usually pays 1:1.
- Blackjack commonly pays 3:2, although some tables pay 6:5.
- Insurance may be offered when the dealer shows an ace. It is a separate bet that the dealer has blackjack.
- Rules for splitting, doubling, dealer play on a soft 17, and blackjack payouts vary by casino or game, so check the table rules before playing.
